01 / About

From learning
to decisions.

How can learning and optimization help us make reliable decisions in a complex, uncertain world?

This workshop brings together researchers working at the intersection of machine learning, mathematical optimization and decision-making. Through invited talks and discussion, we will explore robust learning, optimization under uncertainty, and data-driven approaches to decision-making.
